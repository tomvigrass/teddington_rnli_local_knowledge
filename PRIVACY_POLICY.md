# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-08

## Overview
Local Knowledge Exam does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. All quiz progress and stats are stored locally on your device.

## Information We Collect
We do not collect personal information, analytics, or device identifiers.

## Data Storage
All data is stored locally using the app's on-device storage. You can remove this data at any time by deleting the app.

## Third-Party Services
The app does not integrate with third-party services that collect data.
